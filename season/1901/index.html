1901 VFL Season

The complete 1901 season — final ladder, results and finals.

Premiers

🏆 Essendondef. Collingwood

Essendon won the 1901 VFL premiership, defeating Collingwood in the Grand Final. Geelong had finished on top of the home-and-away ladder as minor premiers.

8 teams contested the season. The 4th-placed cut-off sent Geelong, Essendon, Collingwood and Fitzroy into the finals. St Kilda finished last with 1 win. The full final ladder and round-by-round results follow.
