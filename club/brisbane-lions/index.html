Brisbane Lions — club history & record

Brisbane Lions have won 5 VFL/AFL premierships between 2001 and 2025, the most recent in 2025. The club has also finished runner-up 2 times, losing the Grand Final in 2023 and 2004.

Across 1997–2026, Brisbane Lions have played 700 senior matches in the data here for a record of 361–331–8 — a 52.1% win rate, with a scoring percentage of 102.3. Their biggest recorded victory is a 141-point win over Adelaide in 2004.

By our era-fair rating, the greatest Brisbane Lions players are led by Harris Andrews (100), Lachie Neale (97), Justin Leppitsch (95), Joe Daniher (95) and Simon Black (95).
