North Melbourne — club history & record

North Melbourne have won 4 VFL/AFL premierships between 1975 and 1999, the most recent in 1999. The club has also finished runner-up 6 times, losing the Grand Final in 1998, 1978, 1977 and 1976 and others.

Across 1925–2026, North Melbourne have played 2,139 senior matches in the data here for a record of 913–1207–19 — a 43.1% win rate, with a scoring percentage of 92.6. Their biggest recorded victory is a 141-point win over Richmond in 1990.
